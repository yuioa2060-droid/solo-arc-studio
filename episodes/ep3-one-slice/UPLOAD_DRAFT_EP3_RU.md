## Описание (кандидат)

One slice of cake doesn't break a diet. The sentence you say right after it often does. In this video: the 1980s milkshake study that exposed the "what-the-hell" effect, the weekly math that shows how small one slice really is, and three things you can do in the next thirty seconds so a slip stays a slip.
